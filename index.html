Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the competition

Yes, this is a legal skill-based competition, not a lottery or gambling. Under UK law, competitions that require skill to enter are not classified as gambling. We operate in full compliance with the Gambling Act 2005 and all applicable regulations.

The winner is selected using a certified random number generator witnessed by an independent solicitor. All entrants who correctly answer the skill question are entered into the draw. The draw is live-streamed on our social media channels for complete transparency.

If the minimum number of entries is not reached by the closing date, the winner will receive 70% of the total entry receipts as a cash alternative. This is clearly stated in our terms and conditions, ensuring there's always a winner.

We partner with Yayasan Harapan Bali, an Indonesian charity supporting orphan children across Bali. 10% of every entry goes directly to support their work providing education, shelter, and care for vulnerable children. Visit our charity page to learn more.

Yes, free postal entry is available. Simply send a postcard with your name, address, email, phone number, and the answer to the skill question to: Free Entry, Villa Competition, [Postal Address]. See our full postal entry instructions for details.

The winner receives full ownership of the luxury villa in Bali, valued at £230,000. Alternatively, the winner may choose a cash prize of £160,000. The prize includes all legal fees for transfer of ownership. The winner is responsible for any applicable taxes.

Free Entry

Postal Entry Details

No purchase necessary. Enter by post completely free.

To enter this competition by post without charge, please follow these steps:

  1. Write your full name, postal address, email address, and phone number on a postcard.
  2. Include the correct answer to the skill question: "What is 12 + 3?"
  3. Send your entry to: Free Entry, Villa Competition, 123 Example Street, London, EC1A 1BB, UK
  4. Entries must be received before the competition closing date.

Important: Only one entry per postcard. Multiple entries must be sent on separate postcards. Please ensure your handwriting is clear and include all requested information.
