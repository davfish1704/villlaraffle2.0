Have a Question?

FAQs

Is this legal?
+
Yes. This is a skill-based competition, not a lottery. Answering the skill question correctly is a requirement of entry. Because the outcome depends on a skill element, it falls outside the definition of a lottery under the Gambling Act 2005 — no gambling licence is required. Free postal entry is also available, meaning no purchase is necessary.
How does the draw work?
+
On 9 June 2026, all valid entries (those with a correct skill answer) are collated. A winner is selected at random from the pool of correct entries using a verifiable random selection method. The process is witnessed and the winner is contacted directly by email.
What if not enough entries are sold?
+
If the minimum number of entries is not reached by the draw date, we will extend the competition by up to 28 days. If the minimum is still not met after the extension, a cash alternative equivalent to 75% of total entry fees received will be awarded to the winner instead of the villa.
Who is the charity?
+
We support Yayasan Harapan Bali, an organisation providing care and education to orphan children across Indonesia. 10% of every entry fee is donated directly. Read more on our charity page.
How do I enter by post for free?
+
Write your full name, address, email address and your answer to the skill question on a postcard or piece of paper. Post to: Villa Competition, PO Box [address], [City, Postcode]. One free entry per envelope. Free entries have exactly the same chance of winning as paid entries.
When will the winner be announced?
+
The winner will be announced on or shortly after 9 June 2026. We will contact the winner directly by email and announce publicly on our social media channels. The winner has 14 days to respond and claim their prize.
Can I enter from outside the UK?
+
Yes — this competition is open to residents of the UK, Republic of Ireland and most European countries. Entrants must be aged 18 or over. Please see the full Terms & Conditions for complete eligibility details.
