Yayasan Harapan Bali (Foundation of Hope Bali) is a registered charitable organisation dedicated to providing care, shelter and education to orphan children across Indonesia.

Founded with a simple belief — that every child deserves safety, love and the chance to learn — the foundation has grown to support over 100 children across multiple locations in Bali and beyond.

From daily meals and safe housing to school fees, mentorship and vocational training, Yayasan Harapan Bali works to give children not just a childhood, but a future.

"Every child, regardless of circumstance, deserves the chance to thrive."

When you enter our prize draw, 10% of your entry fee goes directly to Yayasan Harapan Bali. No hidden deductions. No admin fees taken from your donation. Your contribution goes where it matters most.
