About the Charity

Our Mission

Yayasan Harapan Bali (Bali Hope Foundation) is dedicated to transforming the lives of orphaned and vulnerable children across Indonesia. Founded in 2014, we work tirelessly to provide safe shelter, quality education, healthcare, and emotional support to those who need it most.

Our holistic approach ensures that every child in our care receives not just their basic needs, but the love, guidance, and opportunities they deserve to build a bright future.

What We Do

Three Pillars of Support

Our comprehensive approach addresses every aspect of a child's wellbeing

Education

We provide school fees, uniforms, books, and tutoring to ensure every child receives quality education and the chance to build a better future.

Safe Shelter

Our children's homes provide safe, nurturing environments where orphans and vulnerable children can grow, play, and feel truly at home.

Healthcare

Regular medical checkups, dental care, and mental health support ensure every child stays healthy in body and mind.

Impact

How We Help

Every donation directly supports these essential programs

Children's Homes

Safe, family-style residential care for orphans and children without stable homes, staffed by dedicated caregivers who provide 24/7 support.

School Sponsorships

Full educational support including tuition, uniforms, books, and supplies, enabling children to attend school and pursue their dreams.

Medical Outreach

Regular health screenings, immunizations, and access to medical care for children in remote communities who would otherwise go without.

Nutrition Programs

Daily nutritious meals and food support for families, ensuring children receive the nourishment they need to grow and thrive.
